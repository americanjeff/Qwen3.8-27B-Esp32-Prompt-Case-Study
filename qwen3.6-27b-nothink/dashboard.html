LED Circuit:
GPIO4 HIGH → 3.3V on signal wire → 220Ω resistor → LED anode → LED cathode → GND rail → GND pin.

Current ≈ (3.3V − 2.0V) / 220Ω ≈ 5.9 mA
Pushbutton:
GPIO9 has an internal pull-up resistor. When the switch is open, GPIO9 reads HIGH (3.3V). When pressed, the button connects GPIO9 to GND, so it reads LOW (0V).
